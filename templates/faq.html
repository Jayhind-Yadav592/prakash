Frequently Asked Questions

This website is intended exclusively for international buyers — licensed pharmacies, hospitals, clinics, and authorised distributors in their own country — seeking to import pharmaceutical and hospital products from India. We do not accept domestic orders within India through this website, and we do not sell to individual consumers.
We export a wide range of pharmaceutical and hospital products, including injections, tablets, surgical instruments, hospital beds, hospital machinery, and home care products, sourced from reputed Indian manufacturers.
Submit an inquiry through our website, email, or WhatsApp with your business details, the products you require, and the quantity. Our team will verify your eligibility and send a formal quotation before confirming the order.
Minimum order quantities vary by product and are confirmed in the quotation shared with you. Please share your required quantities when submitting an inquiry so we can advise accordingly.
All payments are accepted via bank/wire transfer on a full-advance basis, prior to dispatch. Transactions are ordinarily conducted in US Dollars or another mutually agreed currency; wire transfer charges are borne by the buyer.

Delivery timelines vary by destination country, depending on logistics, shipping routes, and customs procedures at both ends. Estimated timelines are shared along with your quotation.
PRC Surgical provides the commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin for each shipment. Import licenses, customs duties, and any other requirements in the destination country are the buyer's responsibility.
Product listings on our website are for general information only and do not guarantee regulatory approval in any specific country. Buyers must independently verify that a product is permitted for import and use under the rules of their own country's drug regulatory authority.
Each shipment is accompanied by a Certificate of Analysis (COA) and batch details, and products carry a minimum remaining shelf life as mutually agreed before dispatch.
Any manufacturing defect, incorrect molecule, or composition issue is the responsibility of the product's manufacturer, not PRC Surgical. We will cooperate by sharing available documentation (batch details, COA) to support your claim with the manufacturer.
PRC Surgical's responsibility ends once the goods clear Indian export customs. Any delay, loss, damage, or customs issue in the destination country thereafter is not PRC Surgical's responsibility.
Orders cannot be cancelled once advance payment has been received, except by mutual written agreement, in which case costs already incurred may be deducted.
