Established in 2025, PRC Surgical is a trusted trader and wholesaler/distributor dedicated to supplying high-quality surgical and medical equipment. With an unyielding commitment to clinical excellence and supply-chain reliability, we specialize in a comprehensive range of essential healthcare products including pharmaceutical injections, disposable syringes, surgical gloves, infusion sets, bandages, and clinical disinfectants such as Sterillium and Surgiclin Clinical Spirit.

Under the visionary leadership of CEO Prakash Ragaram Chaudhari, PRC Surgical operates from Bhiwandi (Thane, Maharashtra) — the premier warehousing and multi-modal transport hub of Western India. This strategic positioning allows us to dispatch emergency and bulk consignments rapidly with full batch traceability and compliance.

At PRC Surgical, we prioritize customer satisfaction, prompt delivery, and rigorous adherence to the highest standards of safety, sterility, and regulatory compliance. Our extensive manufacturer network empowers us to meet the dynamic demands of hospitals, nursing homes, diagnostic clinics, retail pharmacies, and global medical export partners with complete transparency.
