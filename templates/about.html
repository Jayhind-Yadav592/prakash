Who We Are

PRC Surgical is an India-based wholesale distributor and trading enterprise specialising in the international supply of pharmaceutical formulations, surgical instrumentation, and hospital-grade medical equipment. Headquartered in Bhiwandi, Maharashtra — within one of Western India's foremost multi-modal export logistics corridors — we work exclusively with overseas hospitals, healthcare institutions, and licensed international distributors, delivering dependable access to India's vast pharmaceutical and surgical manufacturing capability.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, PRC Surgical was conceived with a singular mandate: to establish a compliant, trustworthy bridge between India's expansive healthcare manufacturing base and international markets in need of authentic, competitively priced medical and pharmaceutical products. Under the stewardship of Chief Executive Officer Prakash R Chaudhari, the enterprise has cultivated an extensive network of wholesale distributors and manufacturers spanning the pharmaceutical and surgical equipment sectors.

Our Bhiwandi headquarters situates us at the epicentre of Western India's premier export logistics infrastructure, enabling the swift, compliant dispatch of international consignments, underpinned by rigorous batch-level traceability at every stage of fulfilment.

Our Mission

To furnish international healthcare institutions with authentic, quality-assured pharmaceutical and surgical products — executed with precision, transparency, and unwavering regulatory compliance — thereby cultivating enduring, trust-based partnerships across global markets.

Our Vision

To be recognised among the foremost Indian exporters of pharmaceutical and surgical equipment, distinguished by integrity, operational excellence, and an unrelenting commitment to international healthcare standards.

Who We Serve

Overseas hospitals, healthcare institutions, and licensed international distributors seeking a dependable, compliant source of Indian pharmaceutical and surgical products.

Leadership — Prakash R Chaudhari

PRC Surgical operates under the leadership of Prakash R Chaudhari, whose exacting approach to sourcing, compliance, and client relations continues to define the organisation's reputation for reliability.
