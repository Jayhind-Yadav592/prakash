1 Introduction

PRC Surgical ("the Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates this website to serve international buyers seeking pharmaceutical and hospital products for export. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from visitors and business inquirers, how we use it, and the choices available to you. By using this website, you consent to the practices described below.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information that visitors voluntarily provide through inquiry forms, email, or WhatsApp, including business name, contact person, country, email address, phone number, and the products or quantities of interest.

Where an inquiry proceeds to an order, we additionally collect shipping address, business registration or import license details, and payment remittance confirmation. We do not collect or store credit card, debit card, or other financial account numbers on this website; all payments are handled directly through bank wire transfer arrangements communicated separately.

3 How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used to respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, verify buyer eligibility, process and fulfil export orders, prepare shipping and customs documentation, and communicate with buyers regarding the status of their orders. We may also use contact details to share relevant product updates, provided this is consistent with applicable law and any preferences you have expressed.

4 Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade buyer information to third parties. Information may be shared with logistics and freight partners, customs agents, our bank, and other service providers strictly to the extent necessary to fulfil an order or complete a transaction. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, regulation, or a valid governmental or judicial request.

5 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6 Data Retention

We retain business inquiry and order information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, including ongoing business relationships, accounting, and compliance with applicable export and tax record-keeping requirements, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

7 International Data Transfers

As this website serves international buyers, information you provide may be transferred to, stored, and processed in India or in other countries involved in fulfilling your order, including those of logistics and payment intermediaries. By using this website, you consent to such cross-border transfer and processing.

8 Cookies and Website Usage Data

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic website functionality and to understand general usage patterns, such as pages visited and time spent on the site. This data is used in aggregate form and is not used to identify individual visitors for marketing purposes.

9 Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal and business information you have provided to us by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to such requests within a reasonable time, subject to our legitimate business and legal record-keeping requirements.

10 Marketing Communications and Opt-Out

Buyers who receive product updates or promotional communications from us may opt out at any time by replying "unsubscribe" to any such email or by contacting us directly using the details in this Policy. Opting out will not affect communications necessary for processing an existing order or inquiry.

11 Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites, including logistics partners, payment facilitators, or industry directories. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites, and we encourage visitors to review their respective privacy policies before providing any information.

12 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that is likely to risk the rights or interests of affected buyers, we will take prompt steps to contain the breach and will notify affected parties and, where required, the relevant regulatory authority, in accordance with applicable law.

13 Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business-to-business use by licensed pharmaceutical and healthcare buyers and is not directed at, nor intended for use by, children. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 18.

14 Grievance Officer and Applicable Law

This Policy is intended to operate in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable Indian laws governing data protection, as well as relevant data protection requirements of the buyer's own country where applicable.

For any grievance relating to the handling of your information, you may contact our Grievance Officer at: prcsurgical.exports@gmail.com.

15 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. The updated version will be posted on this page, and continued use of the website following any such update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
