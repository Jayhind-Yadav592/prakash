Production & Formulation Standard

Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP / cGMP) Certified

Schedule M & ISO 9001 Compliant

All pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities partner with Current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP), Schedule M, and European EMA guidelines. This comprehensive regulatory framework ensures that every therapeutic compound is synthesized, formulated, tableted, and packaged under sterile, automated cleanroom conditions, guaranteeing zero batch-to-batch variation.

Cleanroom Facilities (Grade A/B)

Sterile formulation suites with positive air pressure, continuous HEPA filtration, and automated particle monitoring.

Batch Certificate of Analysis (COA)

Every production batch undergoes individual release testing and is accompanied by an authenticated manufacturer COA document.

Pharmacopeial Monograph Precision

Exact chemical uniformity conforming to United States Pharmacopeia (USP), British Pharmacopoeia (BP), and Indian Pharmacopoeia (IP).

Tamper-Evident Packaging

High-barrier ALU-ALU foil and blister packaging protecting sensitive active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) against humidity and oxidation.

GMP compliance ensures that medications are consistently manufactured to the highest clinical efficacy thresholds.
International Procurement & Distribution Standard

WHO Certified & Prequalified Norms

WHO TRS 957 Compliant

Our portfolio includes medicines adhering to World Health Organization (WHO) Technical Report Series benchmarks. This prequalification status satisfies international regulatory criteria for procurement by institutional bodies, government health ministries, and global humanitarian organizations across more than 50 countries.

Global Procurement Pre-qualification

Compliant with procurement tenders by international relief bodies, institutional hospital chains, and global healthcare programs.

Good Distribution Practices (GDP)

Rigorous warehouse temperature-mapping and cold-chain transport (2°C–8°C and 15°C–25°C) adhering to WHO TRS 957 guidelines.

Pharmacovigilance & Safety Monitoring

Integrated post-marketing surveillance and immediate adverse reaction notification systems to ensure continual patient safety.

Batch Serialization & Traceability

2D datamatrix barcodes and unique serial numbering preventing counterfeit diversion and confirming authentic factory origin.

WHO guidelines ensure universal healthcare accessibility with zero compromise on safety or therapeutic equivalence.
Laboratory Verification & Analytical Testing

100% Analytical Quality Control & Testing

Multi-Stage QC Protocol

Before any pharmaceutical formulation is approved for inventory allocation, it undergoes rigorous physical, chemical, and microbiological testing in GLP-certified analytical laboratories. These multi-stage assays verify active API content, dissolution profiles, stability under tropical climates, and the complete absence of harmful impurities or pyrogens.

HPLC & Chromatographic Assay

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) quantifying molecular purity, exact active content, and chemical degradation limits.

Dissolution & Bioavailability

In-vitro dissolution profiling testing rate of drug release, ensuring physiological bioequivalence and rapid clinical absorption.

Microbial & Endotoxin Testing

LAL endotoxin assays and sterility cultures ensuring 100% absence of pathogens, bioburden, and pyrogens in oral and injectable forms.

Zone IV Climatic Stability

Accelerated stability testing in climatic chambers (40°C / 75% RH) confirming full 24-to-36 month shelf life under tropical conditions.

Every single batch is validated by independent analytical chemists before release into international wholesale channels.
